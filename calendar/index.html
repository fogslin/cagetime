How it works

  • Subscribe opens your calendar app (Apple Calendar, Outlook) with a webcal:// link.
  • Google Calendar adds the feed by URL; on a phone, open the link in a browser signed in to Google. You can also paste the copied link in Google Calendar → Other calendars → From URL.
  • Events are stored in UTC, so your calendar shows them in your own time zone. Google refreshes subscribed calendars on its own schedule (often every few hours).
  • Each event page also has an Add to calendar button for a single event.
