UFC Card Builder

You're the matchmaker. Pick current UFC fighters from every division, chase the hype, grade your card, then simulate it.

How it works

  • Rules: fighters come from the same division. Different divisions make a catchweight bout, and two champions make a super fight. A fighter can only appear once.
  • Warnings: rematches (from Tapology fight histories), huge rank gaps and possible teammates (same Tapology affiliation).
  • Hype meter: ranks, title stakes, P4P status, finish rates, output, style clash, rematch story and win streaks. Gym-mates and mismatches lose points.
  • Card grade: weighted by slot (main event counts most), with a bonus for title fights.

Simulation results Simulation, not real results

A weighted random simulation from record, rank, finish rates and ESPN UFC stats. Run it again and you'll get different results. It is not a prediction.
