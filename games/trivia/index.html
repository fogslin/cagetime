How scoring works

Each correct answer is worth 100 points plus up to 100 for speed, plus a streak bonus (+25 for every answer in a row after the first, up to +100). The total is multiplied by the difficulty: Easy x1, Medium x1.5, Hard x2. In the daily challenge each question uses its own difficulty multiplier. Best scores are saved on this device only.

Questions cover the men's UFC across every era. History comes from Wikipedia (UFC event results, title histories, records, the Hall of Fame and The Ultimate Fighter), cross-checked where pages overlap; current questions use the official UFC rankings, Tapology UFC fight records and ESPN UFC stats. Every answer links its source. Records and rankings are as of the last weekly update.
