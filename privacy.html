Privacy

Privacy & disclosures

Short version: CageTime has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Your settings and picks stay in your own browser.

What we store

CageTime is a static website. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

A few choices are saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them. They never leave your device, and you can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser:

  • your country for "where to watch" (ct) and your promotion filter, UFC by default (ct-promos)
  • your Pick'em picks (ct-pickem-v1); export and import only happen when you press those buttons
  • your fight-alert choices (ct-alerts) and whether you closed the "install app" hint (ct-install-dismissed)
  • saved games of the CageTime games (Career Mode, GM Mode, Trivia, Card Builder)

Services that see your visit

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host it receives your IP address and browser details when pages load; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts, so Google receives a request from your browser; see the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • OneSignal handles optional fight alerts. Its script loads on the live site, but it is set to require your consent and collects nothing until you press "Turn on alerts". If you do, OneSignal stores your browser's push subscription and the promotions you picked; turn alerts off in the alerts panel or your browser settings. See the OneSignal privacy policy.

Fighter photos are copies of free-licensed Wikimedia Commons files hosted on this site (see credits). Links to Tapology, ESPN, broadcasters, odds sites and promotions take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

There is no newsletter signup yet, so no email addresses are collected.

Affiliate & advertising disclosure

CageTime currently shows no ads, no sponsored content and no affiliate links, and is not paid by any promotion, broadcaster or sportsbook.

In the future the site may earn money from affiliate links (for example to streaming services or tickets): if you buy something through such a link, CageTime may get a commission at no extra cost to you. If that happens, those links will be labelled and this page will be updated first. Money never changes the data: start times, broadcasters, records, rankings and odds always come from the sources named on each page.
